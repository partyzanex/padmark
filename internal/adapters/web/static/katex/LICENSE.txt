Use: static/math.js draws the TeX formulas of a note page and of the editor preview with
katex.render. The files are embedded in the binary and served from /static/katex/ under the
global CSP (script-src 'self', style-src 'self', font-src 'self'), with no third-party origin.

loads woff2, so it never requests the woff and ttf fallbacks.
